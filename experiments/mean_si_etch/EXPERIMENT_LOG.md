## 2026-08-30 — Nested leave-one-lot-out benchmark

Command:

```bash
bosch-vm run \
  --config experiments/mean_si_etch/config.yaml \
  --n-jobs -1
```

Every number below comes from outer leave-one-lot-out predictions. Hyperparameter search in
each outer fold used only the nine training lots.

| Model | Macro-lot MAE (µm) | Pooled RMSE (µm) | OOF R² |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Elastic Net | 0.0921 | 0.1151 | 0.917 |
| PLS | 0.1112 | 0.1415 | 0.875 |
| Cycle-feature Ridge | 0.1239 | 0.1556 | 0.848 |
| Extra Trees | 0.1400 | 0.1640 | 0.832 |
| PCA + GPR | 0.1633 | 0.2101 | 0.724 |
| RBF-SVR | 0.1756 | 0.2209 | 0.694 |
| Context-only Ridge | 0.1958 | 0.2447 | 0.625 |
| Training-mean baseline | 0.3475 | 0.4044 | -0.024 |

Cycle-feature Ridge selected `alpha=10` for the all-data refit and in 8 of 10 outer folds.
Its lot-cluster bootstrap interval for macro-lot MAE was 0.0985–0.1475 µm. Context-only
Ridge was worse on all ten held-out lots; its paired macro-lot MAE was 0.0719 µm higher
(95% lot-cluster interval 0.0457–0.1020).

Elastic Net had lower MAE than cycle-feature Ridge on 9 of 10 lots, with a paired macro-lot
difference of -0.0319 µm (95% interval -0.0503 to -0.0158). That comparison uses the same
outer predictions used to compare the model families, so it is evidence for a follow-up
candidate rather than an independent performance confirmation.

## 2026-08-30 — Sensitivity and uncertainty

- Removing the RF/power feature block increased macro-lot MAE by 0.0519 µm and was worse on
  9 of 10 lots. This measures model reliance, not a causal process effect.
- Replacing `StandardScaler` with `RobustScaler` reduced macro-lot MAE by 0.0188 µm and was
  better on 8 of 10 lots.
- Recomputing the target from direct post-oxide measurements shifted wafer means by
  -0.0403 µm on average and by as much as 0.2881 µm for one wafer.
- Gaussian-process intervals were under-calibrated: nominal 80%, 90%, and 95% intervals
  covered 65.9%, 78.4%, and 84.1% of held-out wafers.
